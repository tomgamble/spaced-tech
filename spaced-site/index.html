WHY
SPACED?
IT STARTED WITH A SIMPLE FRUSTRATION

There should be a better way.

On a busy campus, the last thing you want to do before class is circle parking lots hoping to get lucky. SPACED started with an idea: give students a clearer picture of their options before they leave.

We’re starting at the University of Rhode Island, building around the real rhythms of campus life — with the ambition to make parking less stressful at campuses everywhere.
