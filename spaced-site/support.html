Help & feedback

SPACED Support

We want campus parking to feel easier. Send us a bug report, idea, or question from inside the app.

Send feedback from SPACED

Open the app, choose Account, then tap Send feedback. Select Bug, Idea, or Other and describe what happened. Please avoid submitting sensitive personal details.

Account help

To change your password, open Account → Change password. To start account deletion, open Account → Delete account. If you need additional removal of account-associated feedback or parking activity, contact us through the app before deleting your login while we improve our deletion workflow.

Parking accuracy

SPACED shows estimated parking conditions rather than guaranteed open spaces. Follow official campus signs and permit rules. The app is independently developed, not officially affiliated with URI.

A public support email or contact form should be added before App Store submission so people who cannot sign in can still reach the developer.
