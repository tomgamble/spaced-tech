SPACED U · Data & privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 7, 2026

This policy describes how SPACED U (“SPACED,” “we,” or “the app”) handles information when you use our campus parking application. SPACED is an independently developed service, initially serving the University of Rhode Island.

Information we collect

  • Account details: Your email address, Firebase Authentication account identifier, and profile settings such as selected campus or parking preferences.
  • Parking activity: Your saved or recent destination selections, parking preferences, parking reports, arrival confirmations, reported difficulty finding parking, associated timestamps, and similar interactions. Some records are linked to your account.
  • Feedback and support: If you send us a report or suggestion, we store its content, type, timestamp, and the account ID associated with the submission.
  • Location for routing: When you choose a feature that needs your location, the app requests foreground device location and transmits coordinates to an external route service to estimate a drive time. We do not require continuous background location tracking in the current app.

How we use information

We use information to provide sign-in, personalize campus parking options, estimate travel and parking conditions, support app features, investigate feedback, maintain service security, and evaluate or improve product performance. Parking trends may use modeled data, historical observations, and aggregated community signals; they do not guarantee real-time availability.

Third-party services

SPACED uses Google Firebase services for authentication and database storage. The app uses the public OSRM routing endpoint (router.project-osrm.org) for certain requested drive-time estimates, which receives routing coordinates and associated network request information. Expo and Apple provide app distribution and platform infrastructure. Providers may handle information under their own applicable terms and privacy practices.

Data sharing and tracking

We do not sell user information or use collected information for cross-app advertising tracking. We share data with infrastructure and routing service providers as needed to operate requested functionality, and when required by applicable law. We do not publish users’ email addresses or account-linked feedback publicly.

Data retention and deletion

You can initiate deletion of your account in the app under Account → Delete account. The current account-deletion function removes the Firebase Authentication account and primary SPACED user profile. Other account-associated parking reports or feedback may require a separate deletion request or additional cleanup; do not assume the in-app action removes every stored record. We are working to make associated-data removal comprehensive before public release. Aggregated or de-identified operational statistics may be retained where they cannot reasonably be linked to your account.

Your choices

You can decline foreground location permission; location-based travel-time calculations may then be unavailable. You can stop sending optional feedback or parking reports, and you can request account deletion through the app. For questions about privacy or additional data removal, email supportspacedu@gmail.com or see our support page.

Contact us

For privacy questions, data-access requests, or account-deletion assistance, email supportspacedu@gmail.com.

Security

We use account authentication and access-control rules to help protect stored information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Children

SPACED is designed for campus transportation and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our features or data practices change. The date above reflects the current version.

University relationship

SPACED is not an official University of Rhode Island parking application and is not endorsed by URI. Always follow campus permit restrictions, parking signs, and university rules.

This policy should be reviewed and updated before public App Store submission, particularly the associated-data deletion process and any new third-party services.
