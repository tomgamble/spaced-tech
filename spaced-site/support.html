Help & feedback

SPACED Support

We want campus parking to feel easier. Email supportspacedu@gmail.com for questions, account assistance, bug reports, or feedback. You can also send feedback from inside the app.

Email SPACED support

supportspacedu@gmail.com

You can reach us even if you cannot sign in to the app. For privacy and account-deletion requests, email us from your account email when possible so we can verify ownership.

Send feedback from SPACED

Open the app, choose Account, then tap Send feedback. Select Bug, Idea, or Other and describe what happened. Please avoid submitting sensitive personal details.

Account help

To change your password, open Account → Change password. To start account deletion, open Account → Delete account. If you need additional removal of account-associated feedback or parking activity, email supportspacedu@gmail.com before deleting your login while we improve our deletion workflow.

Parking accuracy

SPACED shows estimated parking conditions rather than guaranteed open spaces. Follow official campus signs and permit rules. The app is independently developed, not officially affiliated with URI.
